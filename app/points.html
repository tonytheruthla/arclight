Weekly creator bounty

Every week we split a pot between the people who made the best case for Arclite.

$500 / week
01Make something original — a post, thread, video, meme, or teardown mentioning Arclite and tagging @arclitefun.
02Submit it below. The form composes a public entry post on X — timestamped, visible to everyone, impossible for us to lose.
03We pick winners every Monday and publish the full list — who won, what they made, what they were paid.
04Quality over reach. A sharp thread from an account with 40 followers beats a lazy repost from one with 40,000.
05Criticism is eligible. If you find a real flaw and write it up well, that's worth more to us than praise.
Entries are public, on purpose. This fills in a post on X for you — we don't run a database, so there's nowhere for your entry to quietly disappear to. A public post is timestamped and visible to everyone, including you, which means we can't lose it, drop it, or claim it never arrived. Your wallet address is visible too, so use one you're happy to have public.
